# Multi-Opponent QC

Load only for one-vs-many scenes.

## Scheduling
- protagonist route defined before enemy attack allocation,
- usually 1 active attacker; at most 2 overlapping immediate threats,
- next threat may enter before current threat fully resolves,
- non-active enemies have a meaningful state: approaching, flanking, blocking, recovering, reentering,
- no neutral turn-taking queue,
- no unexplained full-group rush.

## Spatial continuity
For every relevant enemy:
- location/screen side is plausible,
- lane is known,
- current state is known,
- displacement/recovery persists,
- reentry follows a physically plausible route.

Environment should explain why the group repeatedly compresses into local 1v1 / 1v2 pressure.

## Target switching
The protagonist's new threat focus must be readable through at least one of:
- head/eye orientation,
- chest rotation,
- foot repositioning,
- camera reframe.

## Directing
- protagonist remains visual/spatial anchor,
- current threat is readable,
- next threat may be partial/off-axis,
- camera does not abandon protagonist for unrelated enemy activity,
- after a major route change, group geography is re-established.

## Recovery / sound
- displaced enemies do not vanish,
- downed/recovering bodies remain spatial conditions,
- protagonist damage/failure persists into next beat,
- off-screen threat sound comes from a plausible direction and is synchronized.
