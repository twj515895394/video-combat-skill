# Directing QC

Load only when detailed Directing or a named Style Profile is used.

## Shot function
- every shot has one primary information job,
- framing proves that job,
- partial-body / no-face shots inherit understandable spatial context,
- detail shots preserve established attack direction,
- body mechanics are not hidden before the decisive mechanic is understood.

## Cinematic coverage hierarchy
- full-body wide / medium-wide two-shots are used mainly to explain spatial state, route, major trajectory or environment geometry,
- they are not the default coverage for most of a cinematic duel,
- after geography is understood, coverage should normally shift toward the most informative subject: attacker-only, defender-only, foreground limb, torso mechanics, contact/control, reaction, footwork or result,
- do not return to a wide master after every exchange unless geography materially changed,
- sports-broadcast-style insistence on keeping both complete fighters visible should fail QC unless explicitly requested.

Internal question:
**What does this full-body two-shot explain that a tighter or single-subject shot cannot explain as well?**
If the answer is “nothing new,” replace or remove it.

## Decisive-mechanic proof
- prove the body region that makes the action understandable, not automatically the entire body,
- kick mechanics may need support foot/hip/leg at least once,
- wrist/arm control may only need hands/wrist/forearm,
- torso impact may only need striking surface + receiving torso + structural recoil,
- throws normally need hips/feet/base removal,
- result shots may show only the receiver/environment once the cause is established.

## Tempo by framing
- wide / medium-wide two-fighter shots carry fast, continuous body action rather than slow turn-taking,
- medium close two-person shots remain active unless real clinch/bind resistance justifies reduced speed,
- close/detail shots may emphasize contact, support, reaction, control or environment without slowing the whole duel,
- actual slow motion is not used unless explicitly requested,
- if slow motion is requested, prefer one decisive contact / push-off / near-miss / environment break rather than a whole wide exchange.

Reject:
- both fighters fully visible and slowly alternating one complete move at a time,
- most of the clip held in one static master angle,
- every shot showing both complete fighters and both faces,
- close-up emphasis achieved by simply making the actors move slowly,
- repeated return to full-body two-shot with no spatial reason.

## Continuous exchange
- dense passages may use attack-stream / defense-stream logic rather than isolated move-reset-move structure,
- the next threat can begin before the previous limb fully returns when body mechanics permit,
- defending hands do not automatically return to neutral guard after every contact,
- contact exit may become the next contact start when style/range support it,
- initiative changes only for a physical reason such as miss, capture, redirection, impact or range change.

Reject:
- parry -> hand returns home -> next parry -> hand returns home,
- one complete named move per fighter in alternating turns,
- neutral pose reset between every exchange.

## Subject selection
- attacker-only initiation shots are allowed,
- defender-only reaction/recovery shots are allowed,
- foreground/partial attacker shots are allowed,
- contact-only inserts are allowed,
- control-mechanics inserts are allowed,
- result/environment-only shots are allowed,
- the absent fighter remains spatially inferable through established direction, partial limbs, foreground/background relation or prior anchor.

## Visual anchor
- one fighter may remain the readable mid-ground anchor while the opponent is fragmented into foreground/edge attacks,
- this is valid when it improves readability of sustained defense/redirection/pressure,
- the anchor may transfer after initiative/state change,
- the off-screen/partial opponent must not teleport or change attack side without explanation.

## State-change cut
Prefer cuts when the physical relationship changes category, such as:
- striking -> wrist/arm control,
- striking -> clinch,
- clinch -> throw,
- standing -> wall/railing pin,
- pressure -> receiver-only recovery,
- grounded -> aerial displacement.

After the state change, frame the new decisive mechanic rather than automatically returning to a master shot.

Reject cutting after every named technique when the information type has not changed.

## Attack-right continuity
- the next attack can begin while the previous action is still physically resolving,
- defense may directly load a counter,
- miss may force a landing that becomes the next entry,
- impact retreat may immediately become pursuit,
- no neutral pose reset between every attack right.

## Impact / redirection
- approach is established before contact/result,
- body/material response occurs after contact,
- result follows the same force direction,
- close impact insert does not replace required decisive-mechanic proof,
- borrowed-momentum redirection preserves the attacker's original energy and shows the vector change; a light guide alone does not create supernatural knockback.

## Camera motion
- movement has a target and reason,
- complex body mechanics use simpler camera,
- whip-pan lands on a readable subject,
- landing intercept preserves trajectory,
- no unnecessary orbiting or camera-side crossing,
- camera motion is not used to fake speed when body choreography is actually slow,
- relatively stable camera + fast foreground action is valid and often preferable to constant shake.

## Editing
- cuts happen for information change, physical state change or motivated action,
- cut-on-motion preserves body phase and direction,
- cut-on-impact may move from attack line to result,
- no repeated angle showing the same information unless explicitly intended,
- a spatial anchor is re-established only when geography materially changes or after a deliberately subjective/detail burst,
- shot lengths may be asymmetric; long shots can carry the fastest action and short shots can isolate perceptually heavy detail.

## Environment
- props/architecture exist before use,
- damage follows contact,
- broken state persists,
- debris/dust follows inertia,
- environment interaction changes route, lane, height or recovery.

## Style Profile
If a named profile is loaded:
- transfer abstract mechanisms only,
- do not copy one exact scene or shot order,
- do not redundantly load generic Hong Kong fallback,
- profile must improve staging/rhythm/subject selection without replacing base choreography,
- profile must still obey the universal cinematic coverage hierarchy unless the user explicitly requests sports/training/master-shot presentation.
