# Action Camera

Camera exists to prove the action, not decorate it.

## Shot functions

### Spatial proof
Shows:
- who is where,
- distance,
- obstacles,
- attack direction.

Typical: medium-wide / full-body / stable lateral angle.

### Biomechanics proof
Shows:
- feet,
- hips,
- support leg,
- rotation,
- complete kick/throw route.

Typical: full-body or knees-to-head, restrained camera.

### Impact proof
Shows:
- contact line,
- compression,
- receiver reaction.

Can be close and may omit faces.

### Momentum proof
Shows:
- retreat,
- chase,
- fall,
- wall impact,
- landing,
- range collapse.

Camera follows dominant displacement.

## Camera-body complexity rule

Complex body action -> simple camera.
Simple body route -> camera may become more expressive.

## Useful motivated transitions

- sleeve wipes foreground,
- body crosses frame,
- pillar occlusion,
- kick passes near lens,
- impact cut,
- landing cut,
- change of movement direction.

## Hong Kong action readability principle

A fast sequence can use aggressive reframing and short cuts, but important mechanics still need one readable proof angle before impact/result is compressed.

## Faces

Do not force faces into every shot.

Action can be carried by:
- feet,
- hands,
- hips,
- sleeves,
- torso,
- contact point,
- landing.

## Avoid

- orbiting two fighters during complex exchange,
- cutting before strike path becomes readable,
- hiding feet during crucial kick/throw entry,
- camera crossing action side without visible re-establishment,
- random handheld shake as substitute for impact.
