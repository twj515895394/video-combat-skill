# Grounded Modern Action

## Visual logic
- shorter travel,
- weighty contact,
- practical obstacles,
- restrained camera during complex exchange,
- fast but readable transitions.

## Movement
Favor:
- short entries,
- forearm checks,
- compact elbows/knees,
- wall/body pressure,
- trips,
- controlled falls,
- immediate recovery.

Avoid wuxia elevation unless requested.

## Camera
- shoulder-height or waist-height follow,
- short backward retreat under pressure,
- occasional close contact insert,
- stable full-body for takedowns,
- environmental obstruction for motivated reframes.

## Prompt vocabulary
grounded close-quarter exchange, compact body mechanics, realistic foot traction, short lateral tracking, controlled impact recoil, immediate continuation.
