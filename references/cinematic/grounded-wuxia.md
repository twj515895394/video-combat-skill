# Grounded Wuxia

Wuxia is a cinematic transformation layer. It does not replace the base martial-art mechanics.

## Core transformation

Take a valid base-style action and amplify:
- spatial travel,
- environmental use,
- garment momentum,
- rhythm contrast,
- verticality,
- elegant recovery,
while preserving:
- support,
- push-off,
- facing,
- contact,
- gravity,
- landing.

## Grounded Wuxia movement vocabulary

- short wall/pillar step,
- railing-assisted push-off,
- vault over low obstacle,
- sliding evasion under attack line,
- brief elevated kick,
- rapid level change,
- garment-led foreground wipe,
- landing into immediate continuation.

## Range principle

Base style still determines tactical range.

Examples:
- Nanquan + Grounded Wuxia: rooted close hands with occasional short environmental displacement.
- Northern Long Fist + Grounded Wuxia: larger spatial travel and leg arcs.
- Bajiquan + Grounded Wuxia: explosive crashes through doors/pillars/courtyard geometry.
- Bagua + Grounded Wuxia: curved angle changes without endless spinning.

## Zero-float rule

Every elevated movement:
`support surface -> compression -> push-off -> simple trajectory -> landing -> continuation`

Avoid:
- hovering,
- wire-like pause in air unless explicitly requested,
- both fighters jumping because one fighter jumps,
- magical horizontal flight with no support.

## Camera language

Alternate functionally:
- feet / push-off detail,
- full-body trajectory proof,
- landing/result shot,
- close-range impact.

Not every shot needs faces or both fighters.

## Environment

Use architecture as action geometry:
- pillar for rebound,
- railing for elevation,
- stairs for height difference,
- doorway for compression,
- table/bench for vault,
- wall for stop/rebound.

Environment interaction must be spatially established before use.

## Prompt vocabulary

grounded wuxia movement, short pillar-assisted rebound, railing-assisted leap, fast low sliding evasion, flowing robe inertia, real-time gravity, weighted landing, continuous opponent-facing recovery.
