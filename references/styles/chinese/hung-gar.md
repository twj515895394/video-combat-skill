# Hung Gar — AI Choreography Model

Hung Gar lineages vary. This file captures a grounded southern choreography identity useful for AI video.

## Style DNA
- Strong rooted lower-body structure.
- Forearm bridge/contact is visually important.
- Hand techniques feel compact and heavy rather than flicking.
- Low-line movement and deliberate stance transitions carry style identity.
- Power should appear connected to floor and torso.

## Range Model
mid-close -> bridge contact -> compact hand impact -> rooted continuation.

## Action Cards

### HG-BRIDGE-PRESS
Forearm meets incoming arm, then maintains short structured pressure to open line.
Exit: attacker remains close and opponent-facing.

### HG-ROOTED-PALM
Feet remain planted or take one short stabilizing step; hips/torso drive palm heel forward.
Result: receiver posture shifts while striker maintains base.

### HG-LOW-LINE-KICK
Compact kick targets lower line without large aerial chamber.
Hands remain active for bridge control.

### HG-FOREARM-CRASH
Forearm contacts guard/upper arm with whole-body structure.
Immediately feeds palm/elbow.

## Combination Grammar
- incoming hand -> bridge press -> short palm.
- opponent retreats -> short rooted step -> forearm crash -> elbow.
- low kick disturbs stance -> hand pressure occupies upper line.

## Tactical Failure Conditions
- constant bouncing,
- excessive high kicks,
- acrobatics replacing stance,
- arm-only impacts with dead lower body.

## Camera Proof
Waist/full-body three-quarter angle showing stance and forearm contact.

## Prompt Vocabulary
rooted southern stance, heavy forearm bridge, compact palm heel, low-line kick, short planted step, structured body pressure.
