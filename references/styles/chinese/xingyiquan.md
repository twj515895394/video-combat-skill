# Xingyiquan — Choreography Model

Avoid turning named classical “elements” into mystical effects. Represent them as movement directions and body mechanics.

## Style DNA
- Directness.
- Forward intent.
- Hands and step arrive as one body action.
- Compact rather than decorative.
- Pressure often continues through the opponent's line.

## Action Cards

### XY-DIRECT-ENTRY
Rear leg drives; lead foot advances; hand travels on direct center path.
Exit leaves body closer and still opponent-facing.

### XY-SPLITTING-LINE
Forearm/palm travels forward-downward to occupy or press an incoming line while step advances.
Result: opponent arm/structure is displaced.

### XY-RISING-LINE
Body lifts subtly from compressed legs while forearm/palm travels upward-forward.
Useful under/through guard, not as a giant uppercut.

### XY-DRILLING-LINE
Forearm/hand rotates on a narrow forward path while body advances.
Keep movement concrete, not “energy spiral”.

## Combination Grammar
- incoming hand -> occupy same line -> step through -> second direct attack.
- opponent retreats -> continuous advancing footwork preserves pressure.
- failed first entry -> foot lands forward -> next hand continues without reset.

## Tactical Failure Conditions
- opponent maintains far kicking range,
- choreography becomes static form demonstration,
- excessive mystical wording.

## Camera Proof
Three-quarter medium/full body to show hand-step synchronization.

## Prompt Vocabulary
direct advancing step, whole-body aligned palm, forward-downward forearm pressure, narrow rising line, continuous forward intent.
