# Shaolin General — Broad Choreography Fallback

“Shaolin” covers many routines, lineages and modern performance/competition representations. Use this only as a broad fallback when the user has not specified a more precise branch.

## Style DNA
- Mixed hand and leg vocabulary.
- Strong stance transitions.
- Dynamic level changes may be used.
- Cinematic Shaolin can support broader movement, but the base mechanics must remain concrete.

## Range Model
Choose one primary tactical range per sequence rather than trying to display everything:
- long kick,
- mid hand,
- close elbow/throw,
- sweep/level change.

## Action Cards

Use atomic libraries rather than inventing “Shaolin magic”:
- straight/palm,
- front/side/round kick,
- low sweep,
- forearm check,
- simple trip/throw.

## Combination Grammar
Select one clear identity for the scene:
- hand-pressure Shaolin,
- kick-and-sweep Shaolin,
- stance-transition countering,
- grounded cinematic Shaolin.

## Tactical Failure Conditions
- turning the scene into a forms demo,
- random animal-style naming without mechanics,
- excessive acrobatics,
- mixing every possible Chinese technique in ten seconds.

## Camera Proof
Full-body when stance/kicks/sweeps matter; close inserts only for contact.

## Prompt Vocabulary
traditional Chinese stance transition, direct palm strike, controlled low sweep, clear support-foot kick, grounded Shaolin choreography.
