# Pigua — AI Choreography Model

## Style DNA
- Large arm arcs coordinated with torso opening/closing.
- Long-range flowing power.
- Motions can transition from high to low lines.
- Needs enough space and camera width.

## Action Cards

### PG-DOWNWARD-SPLIT
Arm travels from elevated position down through a diagonal/vertical arc while torso and step contribute.
Do not exaggerate into club-like overhead windmill.

### PG-HANGING-ARC
Arm follows broad curved route around guard line; shoulder remains structurally connected.

### PG-OPEN-CLOSE-TRANSITION
Torso opens with one long strike and closes/reorients for opposite-side continuation.

## Combination Grammar
- downward split forces cover -> opposite broad arc enters side line.
- missed long arc -> torso rotation continues -> stance reorients -> second action.
- opponent closes -> shorten arc into forearm/body contact rather than trying another giant swing.

## Tactical Failure Conditions
- random full spins,
- rubber arms,
- camera too tight,
- broad movements in impossible confined space.

## Camera Proof
Medium-wide/full body with torso and full arm arcs visible.

## Prompt Vocabulary
long splitting arm arc, torso opening-and-closing, broad hanging strike, flowing long-range continuation.
