# Tongbei — AI Choreography Model

Different Tongbei/Tongbi traditions vary. This file focuses on extended shoulder-back-linked striking as a choreography identity.

## Style DNA
- Long relaxed-looking arm extension.
- Power visually links back/shoulder/torso to hand.
- Strikes can feel whipping/snapping rather than stiff.
- Range is longer than compact southern hand systems.

## Action Cards

### TB-LONG-PALM
Shoulder blade/torso advance before or with arm extension; palm travels long direct/slightly arcing route.
Exit: shoulder recoils elastically and opposite side becomes available.

### TB-WHIP-HAND
Torso rotation and shoulder release create fast extended strike.
Do not render as floppy limb.

### TB-BACKHAND-RETURN
After outward strike, arm/torso recovery creates a returning backhand-like line.
Must remain anatomically coherent.

### TB-LONG-PARRY
Extended forearm/palm meets attack earlier in space, redirecting before close range.

## Combination Grammar
- long parry intercepts -> same body extension feeds palm.
- missed long strike recoils -> opposite shoulder/arm releases.
- opponent backs up -> elastic step maintains long hand range.

## Tactical Failure Conditions
- elbows bend/extend randomly,
- arms stretch unnaturally,
- camera crops shoulders so power chain is invisible.

## Camera Proof
Medium/full body, preferably three-quarter, preserving shoulders and hands in same frame.

## Prompt Vocabulary
shoulder-back linked extension, elastic long palm, whipping hand release, extended forearm intercept, recoil into opposite strike.
