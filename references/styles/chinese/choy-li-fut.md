# Choy Li Fut — AI Choreography Model

## Style DNA
- Larger circular striking arcs than compact southern systems.
- Momentum often travels through shoulder, torso and stance transition.
- Long-range hand attacks can bridge distance before close follow-up.
- Circularity should remain purposeful, not random windmilling.

## Range Model
mid-long hand range -> circular entry -> close follow-through -> angle exit.

## Action Cards

### CLF-CIRCULAR-FOREARM/FIST
Support foot and torso rotate; striking arm travels a broad outside-to-inside arc.
Result: guard is contacted/cleared or torso target is struck.
Exit naturally loads opposite side.

### CLF-LONG-WHIP-STRIKE
Arm remains relatively extended while body rotation supplies reach and speed.
AI risk: boneless arm swing; keep elbow/shoulder anatomy readable.

### CLF-ANGLE-STEP
Footwork shifts attacker off centerline while preserving long striking arc.

### CLF-CIRCULAR-TO-COMPACT
A broad first strike compresses opponent's guard; second action shortens into palm/elbow range.

## Combination Grammar
- long circular strike occupies space -> opposite circular hand follows momentum.
- opponent shells -> angle step -> broad strike wraps around guard line.
- missed broad strike -> torso rotation continues -> foot repositions -> opposite hand attacks, no reset.

## Tactical Failure Conditions
- endless full spins,
- both arms flailing independently,
- cramped environment with no room for arc.

## Camera Proof
Medium-wide/full body to show torso/arm arc and foot repositioning.

## Prompt Vocabulary
broad circular forearm arc, whipping long-range hand strike, torso-led momentum, angle step, opposite-side continuation.
