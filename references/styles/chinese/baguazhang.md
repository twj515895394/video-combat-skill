# Baguazhang — AI Choreography Model

Do not render Bagua as endless spinning around an opponent.

## Style DNA
- Curved stepping changes attack angle.
- Torso stays aware of opponent during angle change.
- Palms/forearms redirect while feet reposition.
- Circularity is tactical, not decorative orbiting.

## Action Cards

### BG-CURVED-STEP
Lead foot steps diagonally/curved outside attack line; rear foot follows enough to rebuild base.
Chest reorients toward opponent at exit.
Never end facing away.

### BG-PALM-REDIRECT
Palm/forearm guides incoming limb across center while body steps off line.
Result: attacker is momentarily turned or extended.

### BG-PIVOT-REORIENT
One foot anchors briefly, other arcs; torso rotates only enough to regain facing from new angle.
Purpose: angle, not spin.

### BG-PALM-ENTRY
From outside/front quadrant, palm travels into shoulder/upper torso line as feet establish angle.

## Combination Grammar
- incoming straight -> curved step outside line -> palm redirects -> second palm from new front-side quadrant.
- opponent pursues -> pivot reorients -> pressure passes beside rather than forcing back-facing escape.

## Tactical Failure Conditions
- camera + fighter both orbiting,
- “circle behind opponent” with no response,
- repeated full spins.

## Camera Proof
Stable three-quarter angle; wider framing when curved footwork is the main event.

## Prompt Vocabulary
curved diagonal step, outside-angle palm redirection, anchored pivot, continuous opponent-facing reorientation.
