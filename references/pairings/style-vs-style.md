# Style-vs-Style Choreography

Use only when fighters have meaningfully different systems.

The goal is not to declare which style is better. Build the fight around **conflicting preferred ranges and tactical goals**.

## Pairing template

For Fighter A and Fighter B define:
- A preferred range
- B preferred range
- A entry method
- B entry method
- A range-restoration method
- B range-restoration method
- one vulnerability created by overcommitment
- one believable initiative-flip condition

Then design:
`A tries to establish preferred range -> B denies/converts -> physical result -> A adapts`

## Boxing vs Muay Thai
Conflict:
- boxing wants hand angle/pocket rhythm,
- Muay Thai can interrupt with teep/kick and becomes dangerous in knee/elbow range.

Useful engine:
boxer slips/steps inside kick line -> hand pressure -> Thai fighter frames/clinch/teep restores range.

Do not make either fighter forget their tools.

## Boxing vs Sanda
Conflict:
- boxer wants sustained hand range,
- Sanda can use kicks and standing takedowns when distance/limb capture appears.

Useful engine:
boxing pressure -> Sanda side kick interrupts -> boxer re-enters -> kick/punch contact becomes throw opportunity.

## Kickboxing vs Muay Thai
Conflict:
similar strike ranges, differentiated by clinch duration/control and elbow/knee emphasis.
Use posture and clinch decisions, not costume labels.

## Sanda vs Muay Thai
Conflict:
- Thai clinch/knees vs Sanda catch/off-balance/throw transitions.
Engine:
round kick/teep exchange -> catch or clinch -> one fighter tries to stabilize, other changes base.

## Southern Nanquan vs Northern Long Fist
Conflict:
- Nanquan wants compact close rooted range,
- Long Fist wants extended kicking/striking space.

Engine:
Long Fist drives long kick/extended strike -> Nanquan redirects and compresses distance -> short bridge/palm pressure -> Long Fist angle-steps or uses environment to reopen distance.

This is ideal for grounded wuxia because the tactical contrast is visually obvious.

## Wing Chun vs Long-Kick Style
Conflict:
centerline close pressure vs long-range leg denial.
Entry must be earned through footwork/deflection; Wing Chun fighter must not teleport inside.

## Bajiquan vs Mobile Striker
Conflict:
Baji wants explosive crash range.
Mobile striker tries to force failed entry.
Initiative flip occurs when Baji plants successfully or overcommits into empty space.

## Bagua vs Direct Pressure
Conflict:
direct line vs angle-changing curved step.
Bagua movement remains opponent-facing; do not simply run around behind.

## Throwing style vs Striker
Conflict:
striker wants clean separation; thrower needs contact.
The throw entry must be created by:
- caught kick,
- blocked punch,
- clinch,
- wall pressure,
- overextended strike.

Never make a standing throw appear from no-contact distance.

## Wuxia overlay

After pairing mechanics are valid, cinematic layer may amplify:
- distance,
- verticality,
- obstacle use,
- cloth motion,
- camera rhythm.

It must not erase the pairing logic.
