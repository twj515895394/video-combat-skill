# Impact Inserts

Impact inserts isolate the contact mechanism and physical result.

They are not mandatory for every hit.

## II-01 Fist / Palm to Torso

Frame:
contact point from shoulder-to-waist region.

Show:
- strike entering frame,
- cloth compression,
- torso displacement,
- attacker's wrist/forearm alignment,
- receiver recoil.

Faces optional.

Avoid:
cutting directly to a fist already embedded in chest with no approach.

## II-02 Forearm Block / Check

Frame:
both forearms or shin+forearm.

Show:
incoming limb direction,
contact,
small structural deformation / redirection,
exit direction.

Sound:
short dry contact, not metal clang.

## II-03 Elbow / Shoulder Collision

Use close-medium framing so body mass remains understandable.

Shoulder bump:
show planted support + upper torso compression if possible.

Elbow:
show short path and actual close range.

## II-04 Kick Impact

For decisive kick:
1. wider proof of support and trajectory,
2. optional tighter contact insert,
3. result shot.

Do not make contact insert replace the entire kick.

## II-05 Body-to-Environment Impact

Frame may isolate:
- shoulder hitting pillar,
- back hitting wall,
- foot crushing bench edge,
- hand catching railing.

Show:
body arrival -> material response -> body reaction.

## II-06 Guard Compression

Close shot of:
incoming strike,
forearms/guard compressing toward face/chest,
receiver's elbows/shoulders shifting.

Useful for creating next opening.

## II-07 Miss / Near-Miss Insert

No contact is still information.

Show:
- fist grazing sleeve,
- kick crossing inches from ribs,
- robe displaced by passing limb,
- hair/cloth air response.

Miss must create continuation state.

## Camera behavior

At contact:
- camera may perform one short controlled jolt,
- do not maintain continuous shake,
- do not zoom randomly.

## Editing

Impact insert works best when:
approach is proven before cut,
contact becomes the cut anchor,
result is inherited after cut.
