# Occlusion & Motivated Cuts

Use foreground motion or environment to hide a cut/reframe while preserving action continuity.

## OM-01 Sleeve Wipe

A robe sleeve passes close enough to fill most of frame.

Cut during full/near-full occlusion.

Next shot inherits:
- same limb phase,
- same body momentum,
- same left/right relation.

## OM-02 Body Cross

One fighter crosses lens foreground.

Use body mass as transition surface.

Good for:
switching from relation shot to impact/result angle.

## OM-03 Pillar / Doorway Wipe

Camera tracks until pillar/door frame blocks subjects.

Cut while blocked.

Next angle must preserve actual spatial positions.

Useful in wuxia architecture.

## OM-04 Kick / Leg Wipe

Large leg passes near lens.

Cut as leg covers frame, then reveal:
- defender response,
- landing,
- counter.

High impact but use sparingly.

## OM-05 Dust / Debris Occlusion

Impact creates dust, cloth, splinters, paper or debris.

Temporary visual obstruction can motivate a cut.

Do not:
use debris to hide impossible teleportation.

## Action-match rule

Before cut:
record
- direction,
- limb phase,
- support foot,
- body height,
- velocity.

After cut:
continue from same phase or immediate physical result.

## Why use occlusion

It enables aggressive Hong Kong-style reframing while preserving physical continuity.

It should feel as if the action caused the edit.
