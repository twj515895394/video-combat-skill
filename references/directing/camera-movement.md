# Action Camera Movement

Camera movement follows information and momentum.

## CM-01 Lateral Track

Use:
two fighters moving horizontally, kicks/footwork, pursuit along corridor.

Camera:
moves parallel to action.
Benefit:
preserves attack direction and body silhouettes.

## CM-02 Short Backward Retreat

Use:
fighter pressure advances toward lens.

Camera:
moves backward only enough to preserve readable distance.

Benefit:
creates urgency while keeping faces/body mechanics readable.

## CM-03 Forward Compression

Use:
defender is trapped / distance closes.

Camera:
short push-in or physical step forward.

Do not:
combine with complex fighter spin.

## CM-04 Whip Pan

Use when:
a fast attack, exit or sudden direction change forces viewer attention elsewhere.

Trigger:
actual body/limb movement.

End:
camera lands on a readable new subject/position.

Never use random whip-pan without a movement target.

## CM-05 Landing Intercept

Instead of chasing airborne subject wildly, camera moves/positions toward expected landing area.

Subject enters frame and lands.

Useful for:
qinggong,
vault,
throw result,
fall.

## CM-06 Tilt With Height Change

Use:
jump, stair movement, fall.

Camera:
tilts only enough to preserve subject trajectory.

## CM-07 Low Follow

Camera near knee/waist/ground level follows:
boots,
sweep,
run,
slide.

Useful:
opening beat,
range transition,
footwork emphasis.

## CM-08 Static Impact Frame

For complex contact:
camera remains almost still so action creates energy.

Very useful for:
throws,
elbow collision,
body crash,
environment break.

## Complexity rule

Complex body mechanics -> stable camera.
Simple body trajectory -> more expressive camera allowed.

## Avoid

- 360-degree orbit during active hand/leg exchange,
- camera crossing between fighters,
- long floating gimbal motion that destroys urgency,
- excessive handheld shake,
- lens movement that hides support feet during kick/throw.
