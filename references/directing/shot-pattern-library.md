# Action Shot Pattern Library

Reusable shot cards for fight direction. These are visual modules, not mandatory coverage.

Each shot card has one primary information job.

## SP-01 Relation Two-Shot

Purpose:
establish both fighters, screen sides, distance and attack direction.

Framing:
medium-wide / full-body.

Use:
opening of exchange, after major position change, after POV/detail burst.

Do not:
hold this framing for the entire fight.

## SP-02 Single Attacker Three-Quarter

Purpose:
show attack preparation, body mechanics and forward pressure.

Frame:
attacker dominates 60-80% of frame.
Defender may appear only as shoulder/arm/edge.

Useful:
punch chain, kick chamber, qinggong push-off.

## SP-03 Single Defender Reaction

Purpose:
show defense/recovery without forcing attacker fully into frame.

Attacker may be represented by:
- fist entering edge,
- leg crossing foreground,
- sleeve,
- shadow/motion cue.

Useful:
slip, forearm check, stumble, wall catch.

## SP-04 Attack Toward Lens

Purpose:
subjective threat.

Frame:
fist/foot grows rapidly toward lens axis.

Constraint:
opponent line must already be understood or defender limb remains visible.

Use sparingly.

## SP-05 Fist / Palm-to-Chest Contact Insert

Purpose:
prove decisive torso impact.

Frame:
hand/forearm + receiver chest/shoulder region.

Show:
approach -> cloth compression -> torso recoil.

Faces optional.

## SP-06 Forearm / Guard Collision Insert

Purpose:
show block/check/redirection.

Frame:
incoming limb and defensive forearm dominate.

Show:
contact direction and exit direction.

## SP-07 Foot Pivot Insert

Purpose:
prove biomechanical support.

Frame:
boots/feet and lower legs.

Use:
round kick, hook, body turn, throw entry.

Can open or bridge between body shots.

## SP-08 Push-Off Insert

Purpose:
prove qinggong/jump support.

Frame:
foot plants on floor/pillar/railing.

Show:
surface contact -> knee compression -> extension.

Next shot:
trajectory proof.

## SP-09 Low Sweep Ground Shot

Purpose:
show sweep line and defender foot response.

Camera:
near floor, lateral to sweep.

Defender:
foot lifts/steps; no synchronized jump.

## SP-10 Over-Shoulder Threat

Purpose:
preserve opponent relationship while making attack feel close.

Foreground:
defender shoulder/head edge.

Background/mid:
attacker advances.

Useful:
straight punch, palm, elbow approach.

## SP-11 Defender-Eye / Near-POV Block

Purpose:
attack enters viewer space then is intercepted.

Foreground:
defender hand/forearm rises.

Mid:
attacking fist/foot.

Do not use as literal camera hit.

## SP-12 Sleeve / Robe Wipe

Purpose:
motivated cut/reframe.

A moving sleeve or robe fills frame for a fraction.

Next angle inherits same body state.

## SP-13 Pillar Occlusion Reveal

Purpose:
use architecture for spatial tension.

One fighter temporarily hidden by pillar.
Movement reveals attack/escape.

No teleport behind pillar.

## SP-14 Landing Intercept

Purpose:
make airborne movement readable without chasing wildly.

Camera waits at landing area.
Subject enters frame and lands.

Show:
first foot -> knee absorption -> continuation.

## SP-15 Impact Result Shot

Purpose:
show consequence instead of strike.

Examples:
- receiver stumbles,
- shoulder hits pillar,
- body slides across bench,
- foot catches balance.

Attacker may be partly off-screen.

## SP-16 Environment Damage Insert

Purpose:
prove force and route change.

Subject:
splintering wood, cracked plaster, sliding table, dust burst.

Use only after physical contact is established.

## SP-17 Recovery Detail

Purpose:
show failure/recovery becoming next action.

Examples:
- hand catches floor,
- boot skids,
- palm grabs railing,
- knee compresses after landing.

Next beat grows directly from recovery.

## SP-18 Eye-Line Trigger

Purpose:
show target recognition / incoming threat.

Close:
eyes/head snap toward off-screen motion.

Use:
before sudden attack direction change.

Do not overuse facial inserts in a pure mechanics exchange.

## SP-19 Depth Rush

Purpose:
show attacker moving from background to foreground.

Camera:
mostly fixed or slight retreat.

Use:
long corridor, courtyard, stair depth.

Keeps direction clear while increasing urgency.

## SP-20 Exit / Re-entry

Purpose:
allow one fighter to leave frame during fast displacement.

Rule:
screen direction remains consistent.
Re-entry must follow known route.

Useful:
wuxia chase,
pillar/doorway movement,
fast pursuit.

## Selection rule

A 10-second sequence does not need 20 patterns.

Usually choose:
- 1 spatial anchor,
- 1-2 mechanics/proximity patterns,
- 1 impact/result pattern,
- optional environment or POV pattern.

The same pattern should not repeat without a new information purpose.
