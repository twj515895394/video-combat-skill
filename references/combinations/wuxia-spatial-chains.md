# Grounded Wuxia Spatial Chains

Use only with a selected base style and grounded-wuxia/qinggong cinematic layer.

Purpose: integrate architecture into continuous combat while preserving support, gravity and facing.

## WS-01 Pressure -> Pillar Step -> Lateral Rebound

Trigger:
defender is pressured toward pillar while still facing attacker.

Action:
near foot plants briefly on pillar at low-to-mid height; knee compresses; leg extends.

Trajectory:
short lateral rebound, not upward flight.

Opponent:
stays grounded and tracks landing zone.

Exit:
defender lands one foot first, knees absorb, torso reorients attacker.

## WS-02 Backward Recoil -> Railing Plant -> Forward Return

Trigger:
impact drives fighter backward toward low railing.

Action:
rear foot plants on rail; leg compresses from incoming momentum.

Conversion:
same leg pushes body forward/up in one simple trajectory.

Use:
airborne side/front kick or evasive pass.

Exit:
landing occurs in front/front-side relationship, not behind opponent unless a visible crossing/reorientation is designed.

## WS-03 Low Sweep Threat -> Grounded Slide

Trigger:
broad high/mid attack creates low escape lane.

Action:
defender lowers center, one foot leads while opposite leg trails/folds enough to slide/step under line.

No:
kneeling teleport,
full split unless explicitly intended.

Exit:
defender remains in front-side quadrant ready for close action.

## WS-04 Stair Height Change

Trigger:
fighter retreats/advances onto one or two steps.

Effect:
height difference changes kick/palm line.

Continuation:
higher fighter may press downward; lower fighter may angle to remove height disadvantage.

Do not:
have fighter jump several stairs without push-off.

## WS-05 Table/Bench Vault

Trigger:
obstacle blocks direct route.

Action:
one/both hands or one foot visibly contacts obstacle; hips pass over; legs clear.

Opponent:
tracks route or attacks landing side.

Exit:
vault lands into movement, not pose.

## WS-06 Sleeve/Pillar Occlusion Camera Transition

This is camera grammar, not body teleport.

Action:
sleeve/body/pillar temporarily fills frame.

Cut/reframe:
new angle must inherit exact body positions and momentum from before occlusion.

## Golden rule

Environment amplifies an already valid tactical choice:
- escape pressure,
- reopen range,
- change height,
- shorten route,
- intercept landing.

Never add an environmental stunt merely because the object exists.
