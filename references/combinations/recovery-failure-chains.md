# Recovery / Failure Chains

Purpose: exploit failed attacks, stumbles and landings as choreography fuel.

## RF-01 Missed Punch -> Shoulder Extension

Attacker misses straight punch.
Result:
shoulder extends, weight shifts forward.
Next:
defender counters angle OR attacker converts to hook/frame using real body position.

## RF-02 Missed Kick -> Forced Landing

Kick passes target.
Result:
leg must retract or land.
Next:
- landing foot becomes new lead,
- defender attacks during landing,
- kicker pivots/reorients.

Never reset instantly to original foot placement.

## RF-03 Sweep Avoided -> Raised Foot Landing

Defender lifts foot over sweep.
Result:
one leg is briefly airborne.
Next:
defender deliberately plants forward/back/side, creating a new angle.
Attacker's sweeping leg also finishes its arc.

## RF-04 Impact Stumble -> Recovery Step -> Counter

Receiver is driven backward.
Result:
one foot steps back urgently to catch center of mass.
Next:
that recovery foot can become loaded support for counter/escape.

Do not make receiver regain perfect stance before responding.

## RF-05 Wall/Pillar Stop -> Compression -> Push-off

Momentum carries fighter into environment.
Result:
shoulder/back/hand contacts surface and body compresses.
Next:
push-off, frame, low escape, or opponent pressure.

No magical bounce without contact.

## RF-06 Aerial Landing -> Knee Compression -> Re-Drive

Airborne subject lands.
Result:
landing knee/hip compresses to absorb force.
Next:
compressed leg drives next step/attack.

Avoid landing -> upright pose -> new attack.

## RF-07 Failed Takedown -> Connected Clinch

Shot/throw stalls.
Result:
fighters are still physically connected.
Next:
underhook/body lock/front control/turn/separation.

Do not teleport apart.

## AI value
Failure chains reduce model tendency to:
- freeze after each move,
- restore original pose,
- ignore gravity,
- start a new action from impossible stance.
