# Counter Conversions

Purpose: turn a real defensive action into immediate offense.

## CC-01 Parry -> Open Centerline -> Straight Counter

Incoming:
straight hand attack.

Defense:
short parry/forearm deflection moves attack just outside center.

Changed state:
attacker's striking arm is extended; defender remains balanced.

Counter:
defender's opposite or same-side hand travels through newly opened line.

Compatible:
boxing, Wing Chun-like choreography, Sanda, Karate, modern action.

## CC-02 Slip -> Loaded Side -> Counter

Incoming:
straight punch.

Defense:
head/torso slips outside while feet stay useful.

Changed state:
hips/shoulders load on one side; attacker is extended.

Counter:
straight/hook/body attack from loaded side.

Do not:
make defender teleport one meter sideways.

## CC-03 Kick Evade -> Landing Vulnerability

Incoming:
round/side/front kick.

Defense:
defender steps/angles just outside line rather than jumping.

Changed state:
kicker must retract or land.

Counter window:
- attack during retraction,
- attack planted landing leg/upper body,
- close range after foot lands.

## CC-04 Kick Catch -> One-Leg Balance -> Sweep/Throw

Incoming:
kick reaches catchable line.

Defense:
partial evade/absorb + secure lower leg.

Changed state:
kicker stands on one support leg.

Counter:
style-valid sweep/off-balance/throw.

Compatible:
Sanda, MMA, some cinematic mixed systems.

## CC-05 Shell/Cover -> Angle Exit -> Return Fire

Incoming:
short punch pressure.

Defense:
guard compresses and absorbs enough to survive.

Changed state:
defender's posture is temporarily closed.

Conversion:
one foot steps to angle as guard opens into counter.

Avoid:
instant counter through fully occupied arms with no space.

## CC-06 Sprawl -> Failed Shot -> Front/Clinch Control

Incoming:
takedown attempt.

Defense:
hips move back/down; legs extend; frame stops penetration.

Changed state:
attacker remains low and connected.

Counter:
front head/underhook/body control or separation.

## Rule
Counter timing must originate from the defender's actual defensive exit state, never from a clean reset stance.
