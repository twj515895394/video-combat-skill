# Range Conversions

Purpose: make different styles fight over distance instead of randomly trading techniques.

## Range ladder

outside -> long kicking -> punching -> trapping/pocket -> clinch -> takedown -> ground

A fight does not need every level.

## RC-01 Long Kicker vs Close Hand Fighter

Long fighter goal:
keep 1.5-2+ body-length spacing for kicks/extended attacks.

Close fighter goal:
survive the long line and enter inside the kicking arc.

Grammar:
long kick -> close fighter angles/deflects -> kicking foot retracts/lands -> close fighter steps into shortened range -> short-hand pressure -> long fighter frames/steps to reopen range.

Useful:
Northern Long Fist vs Nanquan,
Taekwondo vs Wing Chun,
Kickboxing vs Baji-like crash entry.

## RC-02 Punching Pressure vs Clinch Fighter

Puncher:
wants pocket rhythm without being tied up.

Clinch fighter:
accepts contact and converts arm/shoulder connection into frame/grip.

Grammar:
punch pressure -> cover/frame -> distance collapses -> clinch control -> knee/turn/throw OR puncher pivots out.

## RC-03 Striker vs Thrower

Striker:
tries to maintain clean separation.

Thrower:
needs contact.

Entry triggers:
- caught kick,
- overextended punch,
- wall pressure,
- failed clinch strike,
- forced shell.

Never:
thrower teleports from outside range into full grip.

## RC-04 Ground Fighter Standing Entry

Ground specialist cannot begin magically on the floor with dominant control.

Use:
hand fight -> level change/body lock -> takedown -> positional transition.

For 10-second AI clips, stop after one clear positional result unless user asks for ground continuation.

## RC-05 Wuxia Range Amplification

Cinematic layer may magnify the distance conversion:
- railing step reopens kicking range,
- pillar rebound escapes close pressure,
- slide under long attack collapses range.

But tactical goal remains inherited from base styles.

## QC
After every range conversion:
- state actual distance,
- ensure both fighters can physically use the next chosen tools,
- ensure feet/torso support that distance.
