# Atomic Clinch / Control Library

Use when fighters are physically connected at close range.

## CL-WRIST-CONTROL
One hand secures wrist/forearm briefly.
Purpose: redirect/occupy, not freeze opponent indefinitely.

## CL-BICEP/ARM-CONTROL
Forearm/hand contacts upper arm to deny strike line.

## CL-COLLAR-FRAME
Hand/forearm controls head/neck/shoulder posture.
Use for Muay Thai-like knee/clinch choreography.
Avoid static neck hugging.

## CL-UNDERHOOK
Arm threads under opponent arm and controls upper torso/shoulder line.
Useful for MMA/wrestling wall/turning mechanics.

## CL-OVERHOOK
Arm controls over opponent upper arm, often as defense to underhook/body lock.

## CL-BODY-LOCK
Arms connect around torso after range is closed.
Needs chest/hip proximity.
Exit can lead to trip, turn or takedown.

## CL-FOREARM-WEDGE
Forearm structure creates space/opens line.
Useful across Chinese close-hand systems and modern close-quarter action.

## Control rule
Every control must:
- arise from visible contact,
- produce a positional purpose,
- transition or release.
No decorative frozen grabbing.
