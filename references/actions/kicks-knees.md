# Atomic Kick / Knee Library

Every kick must define support leg, striking leg, body organization and landing/recovery.

## KK-FRONT-KICK

### Start
- opponent at kicking range,
- support leg under center of mass,
- attacking knee free to chamber,
- hands remain in guard/balance role.

### Chamber
- attacking knee lifts toward target line,
- pelvis stays controlled rather than collapsing backward,
- support knee remains soft, not locked.

### Peak / contact
- lower leg/foot extends forward,
- support leg remains stable,
- torso counterbalances only enough to preserve center of mass,
- head/gaze remains on opponent.

### Recovery / exit
- knee refolds before retraction, OR
- foot lands forward deliberately into a new stance.

Purpose: interrupt / push / strike.

AI risks:
straight-leg lift with no chamber, support knee hyperextension, backward falling torso.

## KK-SIDE-KICK

### Start
- opponent at long kicking range,
- support foot ready to rotate,
- attacking leg free.

### Chamber
- attacking knee rises and folds toward target line,
- support foot pivots enough to free the hip,
- pelvis becomes more side-on,
- torso counterbalances without collapsing,
- hands remain plausible for guard/balance.

### Peak / contact
- heel is the primary force line,
- hip drives through the heel,
- support knee remains stable and not hyperextended,
- head/gaze stays opponent-aware even though torso becomes side-on.

### Recovery
- attacking knee refolds before full retraction OR
- kicking foot lands forward intentionally.

### Exit
- feet/torso reorient into usable fighting relation.

AI risks:
support foot still facing straight forward while hip rotates fully side-on, locked support knee, kick finishing with back permanently toward opponent.

## KK-ROUND-KICK

### Start
- kicking range established,
- support foot able to pivot,
- attacking leg free to chamber/swing.

### Load
- support heel/foot begins pivot,
- hip opens/turns toward arc,
- attacking knee leads the rotational path rather than lower leg whipping independently.

### Peak / contact
- shin/instep crosses target line in one coherent arc,
- support foot has rotated enough to release the hip,
- torso leans only as needed for balance,
- opposite arm/hand can counterbalance while guard remains plausible.

### Recovery / landing
- leg retracts or continues to a deliberate landing,
- pelvis/torso follow the actual momentum,
- no instant reset to original stance.

AI risks:
kicking leg switches side mid-swing, support foot frozen, torso spins independently of hips.

## KK-LOW-KICK

Uses round-kick organization toward thigh/low target.

### Key posture
- support foot pivots,
- hip turns through target,
- shin is primary striking line,
- torso remains balanced over support region.

### Defender response
May check, withdraw leg, shift weight, or have loaded-leg structure disrupted.

## KK-HOOK-KICK

### Start / chamber
- support foot and hip organize as for a high/side-line kick,
- knee chambers before extension.

### Peak
- leg extends past/around target line,
- heel hooks across on return arc,
- pelvis/torso remain connected to support foot.

### Recovery
- knee refolds and foot returns/lands under control.

High AI risk; use only with enough time and readable camera.

## KK-BACK-KICK

Requires explicit turn and visual reacquisition.

### Start
- opponent-facing stance.

### Turn / chamber
- head/shoulder briefly checks target over the turning side,
- support foot pivots,
- hips rotate,
- kicking knee folds beneath/along the body line.

### Peak
- heel drives backward on a direct line,
- torso does not continue into uncontrolled full spin,
- head retains awareness of target line.

### Recovery
- kicking foot retracts/lands,
- body immediately reorients toward opponent.

Never leave fighter permanently back-facing afterward.

## KK-LOW-SWEEP

### Start
- center of mass lowers first,
- support leg bears body weight,
- torso remains balanced and opponent-aware.

### Load / execution
- pelvis rotates around support base,
- sweeping leg extends near floor,
- torso counterbalances rotation,
- hands remain positioned for balance/guard according to style.

### Peak
- sweeping leg crosses the opponent's support-foot/ankle line.

### Exit
- sweeping leg completes the arc and plants or retracts into stable base,
- no magical return to original stance.

Opponent must lift/step or lose base.
Do not make both fighters jump.

AI risks:
both feet airborne, sweep occurring with upright locked torso, sweeping leg teleporting through support leg.

## KK-KNEE-STRAIGHT

### Start
- genuine close range or frame/clinch context,
- one leg becomes clear support leg.

### Load
- pelvis draws slightly back/down or compresses according to style,
- striking knee folds beneath body.

### Peak
- hip drives forward/up,
- knee travels into target line,
- support foot remains stable,
- upper body frame/control manages range.

### Exit
- knee retracts or foot steps down into continued clinch/pressure.

## KK-FLYING-KNEE

### Start / push-off
- one support leg is clearly identified,
- knee/hip compress before extension,
- push-off direction matches intended trajectory.

### Airborne phase
- opposite knee drives forward/up,
- torso remains organized rather than floating independently,
- one main subject airborne by default.

### Landing
- first landing foot is specified,
- knee/hip absorb impact,
- second foot restores base if needed,
- next high-force action begins only after usable support is recovered.
