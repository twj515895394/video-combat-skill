# Atomic Kick / Knee Library

Every kick must define support leg, striking leg, path and landing.

## KK-FRONT-KICK
Support leg stable; attacking knee chambers; lower leg/foot extends forward.
Purpose: interrupt / push / strike.
Exit: retract or land forward.

## KK-SIDE-KICK
Support foot pivots; knee chambers across/along target line; heel drives straight.
Chest may angle but should remain opponent-aware.
Exit must specify retraction or landing.

## KK-ROUND-KICK
Support foot pivots; hip swings; shin/instep travels arc.
Result depends target/check.
AI risk: leg switches side mid-swing.

## KK-LOW-KICK
Round-kick mechanics directed to thigh/low target.
Receiver may check, withdraw leg, shift weight.

## KK-HOOK-KICK
Knee chambers; leg extends past/around target; heel hooks across.
High AI risk; use only with enough time and readable camera.

## KK-BACK-KICK
Requires explicit turn:
front-facing -> shoulder check/rotation -> heel drives backward -> immediate reorientation.
Never leave fighter back-facing afterward.

## KK-LOW-SWEEP
Support leg lowers/rotates; sweeping leg travels near floor in arc.
Opponent must lift foot, step, or lose base.
Do not make both jump.

## KK-KNEE-STRAIGHT
One leg supports; knee drives forward/up from hip.
Requires close range or clinch/frame context.

## KK-FLYING-KNEE
Support leg push-off + opposite knee drives; simple trajectory + defined landing.
Only one main airborne subject by default.
