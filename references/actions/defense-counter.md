# Atomic Defense / Counter Library

Defense must change line, range, posture or initiative.

## DF-PARRY

### Start
- defender remains balanced and opponent-aware,
- parrying hand/forearm stays near a useful guard line.

### Contact
- hand/forearm makes short contact with incoming straight line,
- elbow remains slightly flexed,
- shoulder stays connected to torso,
- contact changes the attack line only as much as needed.

### Exit
- parrying hand finishes on a line that can recover, frame or feed immediate counter.

Do not swat excessively or rotate the whole torso for a small parry.

## DF-FOREARM-CHECK

### Start
- stable base,
- checking elbow slightly flexed,
- opposite hand remains useful.

### Contact posture
- forearm meets incoming limb with structure,
- shoulder/torso absorb or redirect force,
- feet remain ready to adjust if force is large.

### Exit
- checking arm may frame, redirect, trap or recover.

A check is not a frozen pose.

## DF-HIGH-COVER

### Start
- forearms/hands rise around head/upper body,
- elbows remain connected to torso rather than flaring wildly.

### Impact
- incoming force compresses guard slightly toward head/chest,
- knees/hips absorb enough force to keep base,
- defender may step/angle under heavier pressure.

### Exit
- cover opens into counter, clinch, angle exit or continued shell.

## DF-SLIP-OUTSIDE

### Start
- both feet remain usable,
- knees soft,
- head begins on incoming straight line.

### Evasion
- knees/waist/torso shift head outside attack line,
- feet do not teleport,
- spine remains controlled,
- hips load subtly for counter.

### Exit
- head is off-center but body remains balanced and opponent-aware,
- loaded side can feed straight/hook/body counter.

## DF-STEP-BACK

### Start
- facing preserved,
- stance width known.

### Execution
- rear foot moves first,
- lead foot follows enough to restore usable stance,
- torso/guard remains opponent-oriented.

### Exit
- range increases while fighter remains attack-ready.

Do not turn and walk away.

## DF-ANGLE-EVADE

### Start
- attack line is known,
- one foot remains ready to become temporary anchor.

### Execution
- foot shifts diagonally off line,
- second foot rebuilds stance,
- torso/head reorient toward opponent.

### Exit
- defender ends in front-side angle, not unexplained rear position.

## DF-SHIN-CHECK

### Start
- defender balanced on future support leg,
- guard remains active.

### Chamber
- checking knee rises,
- hip turns enough to present shin toward incoming low kick,
- support knee remains soft/stable.

### Contact
- shins meet with both limbs decelerating,
- defender torso stays organized over support leg.

### Recovery
- checking foot replants immediately into usable stance.

AI risks:
standing forever on one leg, knee collapsing inward, checking shin facing wrong direction.

## DF-KICK-CATCH

### Start
- defender first reduces/redirects impact enough to make the leg catchable,
- feet remain under center of mass.

### Capture
- arms secure the lower leg against forearm/body rather than grabbing from empty space,
- defender torso remains upright enough to control balance,
- kicker becomes clearly one-leg supported.

### Exit
- catch transitions immediately to off-balance, sweep, strike, release or reposition.

Only use where style/rules/choreography justify it.

## DF-FRAME

### Start
- close range already exists.

### Structure
- forearm/palm creates space against shoulder/upper torso/head line,
- elbow angle remains strong but not hyperlocked,
- shoulder and torso support the frame,
- feet provide base.

### Exit
- frame creates distance, angle, clinch turn or strike lane.

## Counter grammar

A counter starts from the actual defensive exit state.
Never write defense -> neutral reset -> counter.

Examples:
- parry finishes outside centerline -> opposite hand enters the opened line,
- slip loads hip/shoulder -> counter launches before head returns to center,
- check replants -> supporting leg immediately drives next step,
- frame creates space -> elbow/palm/knee follows from the same structure.
