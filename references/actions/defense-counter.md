# Atomic Defense / Counter Library

Defense must change line, range, posture or initiative.

## DF-PARRY
Short hand/forearm contact changes incoming straight line.
Do not swat excessively.

## DF-FOREARM-CHECK
Forearm meets limb/attack line with structure; redirects or absorbs enough to change next state.

## DF-HIGH-COVER
Forearms/hands compress around head/upper body.
Impact should move posture/guard slightly.
Exit can feed clinch/counter/angle.

## DF-SLIP-OUTSIDE
Head/torso shifts outside straight punch line via knees/waist.
Feet remain available.
Exit naturally loads counter.

## DF-STEP-BACK
Rear foot moves first; lead follows.
Range increases but facing remains.

## DF-ANGLE-EVADE
Foot shifts diagonally off attack line.
Exit creates front-side angle, not unexplained rear position.

## DF-SHIN-CHECK
Defender balances on one leg, raises opposite knee/shin into low-kick line.
Must replant immediately after impact.

## DF-KICK-CATCH
Partial evade/absorb + arms secure leg against body/forearm.
Receiver of catch becomes one-leg balanced.
Only use where style/rules/choreography justify it.

## DF-FRAME
Forearm/palm creates structural space against shoulder/upper torso/head line.
Used in clinch/close-range transitions.

## Counter grammar
A counter starts from the actual defensive exit state.
Never write defense then magically reset before counter.
