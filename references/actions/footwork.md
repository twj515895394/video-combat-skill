# Atomic Footwork Library

Load only when exact stepping mechanics matter.

## FW-ADVANCE
Purpose: close distance without crossing feet.
Mechanics: lead foot advances first; rear foot follows enough to rebuild stance width.
Exit: chest remains opponent-facing.
AI risk: rear foot slides without weight transfer.

## FW-RETREAT
Purpose: preserve range.
Mechanics: rear foot moves first; lead foot follows while guard/facing remains active.
AI risk: fighter turns and walks away.

## FW-DIAGONAL-OUT
Purpose: leave centerline while preserving attack angle.
Mechanics: lead or rear foot steps 20-45 degrees off line; second foot rebuilds base.
Exit: torso reorients opponent.
AI risk: instant side teleport.

## FW-PIVOT
Purpose: change angle around a planted reference foot.
Mechanics: anchor foot rotates on ball/heel as appropriate; other foot arcs; torso follows.
Exit: opponent remains in front/front-side quadrant.
AI risk: 180-degree accidental back-facing.

## FW-STEP-THROUGH
Purpose: convert a missed/long strike into forward stance.
Mechanics: striking-side foot lands forward and becomes new lead.
Useful for continuous choreography.

## FW-LEVEL-DROP
Purpose: lower center for body strike/takedown/evasion.
Mechanics: knees/hips flex; spine/torso remains controlled.
AI risk: torso collapses without foot support.

## FW-CURVED-STEP
Purpose: angle change for Bagua/cinematic movement.
Mechanics: diagonal-curved lead step; rear foot follows; chest continuously reorients.
Not a full orbit.
