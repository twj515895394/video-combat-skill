# Atomic Hand / Upper-Limb Strikes

These are biomechanical primitives. Style files decide when/how they are used.

## HS-LEAD-STRAIGHT
Start: lead hand/foot relationship established.
Mechanics: small floor pressure; lead shoulder advances; hand travels direct line.
Exit: hand retracts or remains as frame depending style.

## HS-REAR-STRAIGHT
Mechanics: rear foot/hip/shoulder contribute to direct strike.
Miss: weight/shoulder may become extended.
Exit: hook, angle, or recovery.

## HS-HOOK
Mechanics: hip/torso rotate; elbow/fist travel arc.
Define lead/rear side and target level.
AI risk: arm-only windmill.

## HS-UPPERCUT
Mechanics: knees/hip compress; fist travels short upward path at close range.
Avoid exaggerated vertical launch.

## HS-PALM-HEEL
Mechanics: palm heel drives short/direct into torso/head line with wrist aligned.
Useful for Chinese martial arts/wuxia.
Result: cloth/body compression + posture shift.

## HS-FOREARM-STRIKE
Short path using forearm bone/structure at close range.
Must have genuine proximity.

## HS-HORIZONTAL-ELBOW
Short torso rotation; elbow travels horizontal arc.
Close-range only.

## HS-DOWNWARD-ELBOW
Arm raises only enough to create short downward line; body weight contributes.
Avoid giant overhead theatrical wind-up unless cinematic layer asks.

## HS-SHOULDER-BUMP
Requires body contact.
Support foot plants; hips/body drive shoulder into torso.
Receiver displacement should be modest and weighty.
