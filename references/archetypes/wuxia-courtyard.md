# Wuxia Courtyard Fight

## Engine
A grounded Chinese martial-arts duel expands through courtyard architecture and short qinggong bursts.

Typical geometry:
- stone floor,
- pillar line,
- low railing,
- stairs,
- doorway,
- bench/table,
- roof edge only if needed.

## 3-phase structure
1. Grounded style contrast:
establish who wants which range.

2. Spatial amplification:
pressure reaches pillar/rail/stairs and one fighter uses environment to change distance or height.

3. Re-entry:
airborne/raised movement lands back into grounded combat and the fight continues.

## Base requirement
Must load actual Chinese base style(s).

“Wuxia” alone is insufficient.

## Useful combinations
- range-conversions
- wuxia-spatial-chains
- recovery-failure-chains

## Directing
- relation full-body anchor,
- push-off insert,
- trajectory proof,
- attack-to-camera or impact insert,
- landing intercept,
- environment result.

## Style-profile compatibility
Good matches:
- new-wave Hong Kong wuxia,
- King Hu rhythmic widescreen,
- Yuen Woo-ping prop/tempo,
depending requested tone.

## Failure modes
- two fighters constantly airborne,
- pillar/rail moves location,
- qinggong has no push-off,
- environmental stunt replaces martial-art identity.
