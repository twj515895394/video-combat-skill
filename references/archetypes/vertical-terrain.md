# Vertical Terrain Fight

## Engine
Height difference becomes tactical information.

Examples:
stairs, platforms, roof levels, balcony, slope.

## 3-phase structure
1. Establish height advantage.
2. Attack forces level change.
3. Height flips, equalizes or creates a leap/landing transition.

## Tactical effects
Higher fighter:
- downward pressure,
- longer visual line,
- easier retreat upward/back.

Lower fighter:
- angle around,
- attack support leg,
- use rail/wall,
- force descent.

## Useful combinations
- range-conversions
- recovery-failure-chains
- wuxia-spatial-chains

## Directing
- low/high angle contrast,
- tilt with height change,
- landing intercept,
- stair line as composition anchor.

## Failure modes
- camera loses which fighter is higher,
- jump has no support,
- next cut reverses stair direction,
- height becomes decorative rather than tactical.
