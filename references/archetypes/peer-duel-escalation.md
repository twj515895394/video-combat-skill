# Peer Duel Escalation

## Engine
Two skilled fighters are close in ability. Each exchange reveals information and changes tactics.

## 3-phase structure
1. Fast diagnostic exchange: one or two probes establish style contrast.
2. Adaptation: each fighter counters the other's preferred range/tool.
3. Escalation: both commit more strongly, producing first decisive displacement or initiative swing.

## Rule
Do not spend half the clip posing or circling.
“Testing” still needs real contact, near-miss, parry or footwork consequence.

## Useful combinations
- counter-conversions
- range-conversions
- recovery-failure-chains

## Directing
- relation two-shot,
- technique proof,
- brief reaction/eye trigger,
- alternating detail and full-body proof.

## Failure modes
- equal skill becomes symmetrical mirrored choreography,
- fake pause before every exchange,
- random escalation with no learned adaptation.
