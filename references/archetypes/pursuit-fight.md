# Pursuit Fight

## Engine
Combat and locomotion are fused. One fighter pursues while the other escapes, repositions or counterattacks without stopping.

## 3-phase structure
1. Chase ignition: attacker gains pursuit line.
2. Moving exchange: strikes occur during running, cornering, vaulting, turning or obstacle navigation.
3. Route disruption: defender changes route / height / obstacle, creating temporary reversal or renewed chase.

## Initiative
Pursuer usually owns macro initiative.
Defender may win micro-exchanges while still retreating.

## Spatial behavior
Essential:
- exits and re-entries,
- foreground passes,
- depth rush,
- lateral run,
- corner turn,
- obstacle transition.

## Useful combinations
- pressure-chains
- recovery-failure-chains
- wuxia-spatial-chains

## Useful directing
- depth rush,
- lateral tracking,
- whip-pan,
- pillar/door occlusion,
- landing intercept.

## Failure modes
- characters stop to trade combinations,
- chase direction flips under cuts,
- teleporting around corners,
- both run side-by-side without tactical relation.
