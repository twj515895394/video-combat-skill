# Boss Escalation Fight

## Engine
A stronger opponent forces the protagonist to adapt through phases.

## 3-phase structure
1. Normal tools fail.
2. Boss demonstrates superior control/power/range.
3. Protagonist changes tactic or environment to create a narrow opening.

## Initiative
Boss may dominate macro initiative.
Protagonist earns brief reversals through adaptation, not arbitrary fairness.

## Escalation dimensions
Choose 1-2:
- range,
- speed,
- impact,
- environment control,
- clinch/throw dominance,
- verticality.

Do not escalate everything at once.

## Directing
- establish boss spatial authority,
- impact result,
- protagonist recovery detail,
- environment consequence,
- clear tactical adjustment.

## Failure modes
- boss superiority only expressed by bigger knockback,
- protagonist suddenly becomes equally strong with no change,
- repeated identical attack loops.
