# Pressure Duel

## Engine
One fighter continuously owns initiative and forces the other to give ground, change angle, cover or use environment.

## 3-phase structure
1. Entry pressure: attacker establishes threat and closes space.
2. Compression: defender loses preferred range / gets pushed toward obstacle or edge.
3. Counter-pressure or forced escape: defender either creates one real reversal or barely escapes while pressure continues.

## Initiative
Default:
Attacker retains initiative until:
- overcommitment,
- missed strike,
- environmental obstruction,
- caught limb,
- failed landing,
- balance loss.

## Spatial behavior
Movement should travel through space:
- corridor,
- courtyard edge,
- wall,
- railing,
- stairs.

Avoid stationary trading.

## Useful combination refs
- pressure-chains
- recovery-failure-chains
- range-conversions

## Useful directing
- short backward camera retreat,
- lateral track,
- impact result,
- environment collision,
- exit/re-entry.

## Failure modes
- attacker pauses after every strike,
- defender waits politely,
- both remain centered in frame,
- no actual territorial change.
