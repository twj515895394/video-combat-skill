# Confined-Space Fight

## Engine
Limited room changes which tools are viable.

Examples:
corridor, narrow room, doorway, train compartment, kitchen aisle.

## 3-phase structure
1. Space denial: long attacks are restricted.
2. Contact compression: short hands, elbows, frames, clinch or wall contact dominate.
3. Escape/reversal: one fighter uses doorway, wall, furniture or angle to create space.

## Initiative
Often changes when one fighter pins the other against a surface.

## Spatial behavior
- small steps,
- body turns,
- wall pressure,
- doorframe obstruction,
- short rebounds.

Avoid:
large spinning kicks,
huge qinggong arcs,
wide circular techniques without space.

## Useful combinations
- pressure-chains
- clinch-throw-transitions
- counter-conversions

## Useful directing
- tight medium,
- torso mechanics,
- impact insert,
- obstacle occlusion,
- short handheld/reframe.

## Failure modes
- impossible full-extension kick in 1m corridor,
- camera physically passing through fighters,
- repeated orbit shots,
- environment dimensions changing.
