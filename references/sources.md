# Source Anchors

This file records authoritative domain anchors used to keep taxonomy and sport boundaries grounded.

It is not automatically loaded for normal prompt generation.

## Wushu / Sanda
International Wushu Federation (IWUF)
- Taolu overview and major categories, including Changquan and Nanquan.
- Current Wushu Taolu and Sanda competition-rule publications.

Useful for:
- official category terminology,
- broad competitive Wushu boundaries,
- rule context.

Do not treat competition descriptions as a complete traditional-school technique manual.

## Boxing
World Boxing
- competition rules and technical governance documents.

Useful for:
- sport boundaries and competition context.

## Kickboxing
WAKO
- Low Kick, Full Contact and K1-family rule documents.

Useful for:
- allowed discipline boundaries and distinctions.

## MMA
International Mixed Martial Arts Federation (IMMAF)
- amateur MMA competition rules and rule framework.

Useful for:
- range/domain boundaries and legal competition context.

## Wrestling / Grappling
United World Wrestling (UWW)
- Olympic wrestling and grappling rules.

Useful for:
- wrestling/grappling domain boundaries and terminology.

## Source policy

Reference files contain three kinds of material:

1. domain facts / sport boundaries,
2. choreography-oriented biomechanical abstractions,
3. cinematic transformations.

Never present (2) or (3) as if an official federation published them.

Traditional martial arts vary by school, lineage, competition format and instructor. The library intentionally stores a useful AI-video choreography model rather than claiming one definitive historical or technical truth.
