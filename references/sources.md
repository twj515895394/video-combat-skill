# Source Anchors

This file records authoritative domain anchors used to keep taxonomy and sport boundaries grounded.

It is not automatically loaded for normal prompt generation.

## Wushu / Sanda
International Wushu Federation (IWUF)
- Taolu overview and major categories, including Changquan and Nanquan.
- Current Wushu Taolu and Sanda competition-rule publications.

## Boxing
World Boxing
- competition rules and technical governance documents.

## Kickboxing
WAKO
- Low Kick, Full Contact and K1-family rule documents.

## MMA
International Mixed Martial Arts Federation (IMMAF)
- amateur MMA competition rules and rule framework.

## Wrestling / Grappling
United World Wrestling (UWW)
- Olympic wrestling and grappling rules.

## Judo
International Judo Federation (IJF)
- Sport and Organisation Rules / refereeing rules.
- official technique-name and education materials where relevant.

## Taekwondo
World Taekwondo (WT)
- Competition Rules & Interpretation.
- useful for competition boundaries and legal target/scoring context.

## Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu
International Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Federation (IBJJF)
- official Rule Book and rules education materials.
- useful for competition boundaries, positions, scoring and legal-technique context.

## Sambo
International SAMBO Federation (FIAS)
- separate International Rules for Sport SAMBO and Combat SAMBO.
- useful for preventing accidental mixing of the two variants.

## Karate
World Karate Federation (WKF)
- current competition rules should be checked when exact sport legality or scoring matters.

## Savate
International Savate Federation (FISav)
- current competition documents should be checked when exact discipline legality matters.

## Source policy

Reference files contain:
1. domain facts / sport boundaries,
2. choreography-oriented biomechanical abstractions,
3. cinematic transformations.

Never present (2) or (3) as if an official federation published them.

Rules confirm sport boundaries; they do not prove one universal technical execution.

Traditional martial arts vary by school, lineage, competition format and instructor. The library intentionally stores a useful AI-video choreography model rather than claiming one definitive historical or technical truth.
